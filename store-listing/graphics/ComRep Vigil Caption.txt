🙏 Introducing ComRep Vigil — a prayer companion for the Communion of Reparation All Night Vigil

For those who keep the First Friday–First Saturday devotion in honor of the Sacred Heart of Jesus and the Immaculate Heart of Mary, you know the vigil: hours of prayer through the night, following the booklet page by page — the Exposition, the Holy Rosary, the Nine Offices of the Sacred Heart, Benediction, the Saturday Office of Readings, and more, all the way through the Seven Sorrows of Mary.

ComRep Vigil turns that printed booklet into a guided app you can follow step by step, even in a dark chapel — readings, rubrics, and responses laid out clearly in order, remembering right where you left off if you need to pause. No account, no ads, no tracking. Just the prayer guide.

We're getting ready to launch on the Play Store, and before we do, we need a small group of beta testers to try it out first. If you pray this vigil — or know someone who does — we'd be grateful for your help.
