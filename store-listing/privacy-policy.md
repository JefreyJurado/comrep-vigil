# Privacy Policy — ComRep Vigil

**Last updated: [DATE]**

ComRep Vigil ("the App") is a devotional prayer guide for the Communion of
Reparation All Night Vigil. This policy explains what data the App does and
does not handle.

## Summary

The App does not collect, transmit, store on any server, sell, or share any
personal data. Everything the App remembers is kept only on your own device.

## What the App stores on your device

- **Your place in the vigil** — so you can pick up where you left off. Stored
  locally using your device's standard app storage.
- **Your "Keep Screen Awake" preference** — a simple on/off setting, stored
  the same way.

Neither of these ever leaves your device. The App has no server, no account
system, no login, and no analytics or tracking of any kind.

## What the App does not do

- It does not require or request an account.
- It does not collect your name, email, location, contacts, or any other
  personal information.
- It does not use advertising or advertising identifiers.
- It does not use analytics, crash reporting, or any third-party tracking
  SDKs.
- It does not access the camera, microphone, contacts, or any other
  sensitive device permission.
- It makes no network requests during normal use.

## Third-party services

The App does not integrate any third-party services that collect data.

## Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including
children, because it does not collect information from anyone at all.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes (for example, if a future version of the App
adds a new feature that touches data), this page will be updated and the
"Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
