## Full description (4000 char max)

ComRep Vigil is a guided companion for the Communion of Reparation All
Night Vigil — the First Friday–First Saturday devotion in honor of the
Sacred Heart of Jesus and the Immaculate Heart of Mary, prayed by members
of the Secular Male Institute of the Two Hearts and the wider Two Hearts
community.

Rather than flipping through a printed booklet in the dark of an all-night
vigil, open the app and step through the entire prayer guide one section
at a time — readings, rubrics, and responses laid out clearly, in order,
from the opening Exposition all the way through the Seven Sorrows of Mary.

WHAT'S INSIDE

• Exposition, Salutation, and the Holy Rosary
• Self-Offering before the Blessed Sacrament
• The Nine Offices of the Sacred Heart, in full — Worshipper, Lover,
  Victim, Disciple, Faithful Servant, Suppliant, Promoter, Zealous
  Friend, and Reparator — each with its Scripture reading, motivation,
  and adoration prayer
• Benediction of the Blessed Sacrament
• Saturday Office of Readings
• Act of Consecration to the Holy Family of Nazareth
• A guide for Confession
• The Twelve Promises of the Sacred Heart, the Nine Thorns, the Five
  Offenses against the Immaculate Heart, and the Seven Sorrows of Mary

DESIGNED FOR PRAYING, NOT JUST READING

• A step-by-step guided walkthrough that remembers where you left off
• A full table of contents to jump straight to any part of the vigil
• Rubrics (stand, kneel, sit) and responses set apart from the readings
  so it's easy to follow along, even in a low-lit chapel
• An optional "keep screen awake" setting for the length of the vigil
• No account, no ads, no tracking — just the prayer guide

This app does not collect any personal data. See the privacy policy for
details.

ComRep Vigil is an unofficial, independently developed companion app and
is not affiliated with any diocese. It follows the text of the Communion
of Reparation All Night Vigil Prayer Guide, First Revised Edition,
published by the Secular Male Institute of the Two Hearts.
